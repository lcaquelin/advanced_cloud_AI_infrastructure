# Project template

Copy this folder into your group's repository, then replace the questions with your answers. Keep the headings.

```
your-repo/
  PLAN.md                  your business case, one page, Mon 16 Nov (not graded); then three lines per session
  packaged_app/            your product: docker-compose.yml, README.md, the service folders you kept
  docs/BUSINESS.md
  docs/ARCHITECTURE.md     the functional view, the system view, the detailed architecture
  docs/DECISIONS.md        your numbers live here too
  docs/OPERATIONS.md
  docs/CLOUD.md
  world/                   copied from the course, unchanged (the compose file reads it as ../world)
  platform/hub/            only if you keep the Control Room (read as ../platform/hub)
```

| Part | Owner (write a name) |
| --- | --- |
| `packaged_app/` (the product and its README) | ........ |
| `docs/BUSINESS.md` | ........ |
| `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/CLOUD.md` | ........ |
| `docs/OPERATIONS.md` | ........ |

`docs/DECISIONS.md` is shared: each member writes the decisions of their part. Each member owns one part and
answers the question on it at the oral. The curveballs are not a file: prepare them as a group (`PROJECT.md`,
section 10), one is picked live at the oral.

Start with [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md): your business case, one page, by Mon 16 Nov, before session 3. Then
[`packaged_app/README.md`](packaged_app/README.md): how to make your product start with one command.

See `PROJECT.md` at the root of the course for the calendar and the oral.
